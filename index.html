02 — Method

A disciplined path from idea to production.

Predictable delivery isn’t luck — it’s process. Here’s how a Gideon’s Chosen engagement runs, every time.

  1. STEP 01

    Discovery

    We pressure-test scope, risks, and success metrics before a line of code. You leave with a plan, not a guess.

  2. STEP 02

    Design

    Architecture and interface decided together. Prototypes validate the hard parts early, while change is still cheap.

  3. STEP 03

    Build

    Two-week increments, demoable every Friday, trunk-based and CI-gated. You watch real software take shape.

  4. STEP 04

    Launch & Support

    Hardened rollout, runbooks, monitoring, and an SLA. We stay on past go-live — because that’s when it counts.
